Frequently asked questions

Can you connect to existing manufacturing equipment?

Yes, we provide IoT gateways with adapters for OPC-UA, MQTT, Modbus, Profibus, and proprietary protocols to connect legacy equipment to our cloud platform without replacement.

How does predictive maintenance work?

Our AI analyzes vibration, temperature, and operational sensor data using LSTM models to predict equipment failures 24–72 hours before they occur, enabling planned maintenance.

Is real-time monitoring available?

Yes, we provide live OEE dashboards showing real-time production metrics, equipment status, quality reject rates, and energy consumption with configurable alerts.

Can you integrate with our existing ERP system (SAP, Oracle)?

Yes, we build integrations with SAP MES, Oracle Manufacturing Cloud, and custom ERP systems for real-time production order and material data exchange.

How do you monitor energy consumption across the factory?

We integrate with smart meters and SCADA energy management systems to track consumption per machine, line, and shift — enabling targeted 20–30% energy cost reductions.

Do we need to replace our machines?

No. Edge gateways read most existing equipment over standard industrial protocols.

Where does the data go?

Wherever you choose: on-premises, your cloud account or a managed environment.
