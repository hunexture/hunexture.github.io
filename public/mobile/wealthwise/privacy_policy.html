Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the Wealth Wise application (the "Service") and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

Core Privacy Promise: Wealth Wise is designed as an Offline-First application. Your data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your personal records to any external cloud servers.

Interpretation and Definitions

Company (referred to as either "the Company", "We", "Us" or "Our" in this Agreement) refers to Huntexture.

Service refers to the Wealth Wise mobile application.

Personal Data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

1. User-Inputted Data (Locally Stored)

To provide our expense tracking and calculator features, you may input data such as:

  • Transaction amounts and categories
  • Budget limits
  • Loan principal and interest rates
  • Credit Card names and limits

Important: We do NOT collect or store sensitive banking information such as full Credit Card numbers, CVV, Bank Account numbers, or Net Banking passwords.

How we use it: This data is processed strictly within the app on your device to generate charts and calculations. It is NOT transmitted to Us.

2. Usage Data

We may collect minimal, anonymous usage data to understand how the app is used (e.g., which calculators are most popular) to improve the Service. This does not include your financial figures.

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Storage: To save your financial data database locally on your device so it persists between app sessions.

Security of Your Data

Since your data is stored locally, the security of your data largely depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your device password-protected to prevent unauthorized access to your records.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
